AboutKLB Komaki Private limited

Komaki Group has registered a staggering overall growth rate of 1200 per cent within 20 months of its inception in May 2020 with a turnover of Rs 301 crores in March 2022. Notably, the exemplary growth of Komaki Group is completely organic, without any dependency on government subsidy and without any bank loans or funding from any financial institutions. This has also placed Komaki Electric among one of the few start ups in the clean energy sector which is running into profit consistently.

On the company’s staggering growth and future plans, Gunjan Malhotra, Director of Komaki Group, said, “Komaki, in just few years of existence, has earned a great reputation in the market and financially too the company is experiencing rapid top-line as well as bottom-line growth without any external funding. The brand has established a strong network of 380+ dealership showrooms all over the country. We offer the widest range of electric 2-wheelers, with 9 CMVR-exempt models and 8 high-speed registration models. Besides, we are working on the application of advanced technologies to manufacture the best-in-the-market LIFEPO4 batteries for our EVs.

Komaki Electric has state-of-the-art manufacturing plant spanning over five acres with offices and warehouse in Delhi / NCR.
